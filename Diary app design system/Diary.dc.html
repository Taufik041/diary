Tuesday, 8 September
Slept badly and woke before the alarm, so I made coffee and sat on the back step until the neighbours' cat came over. The whole garden still smells of last night's rain. I keep thinking about Sunday — how quiet it got past the second field, and how we stopped for twenty minutes to watch a heron do absolutely nothing at all.
